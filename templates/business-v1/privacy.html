Privacy

This is the NXQ-Web baseline privacy surface. The business remains responsible for reviewing any industry-, location-, or provider-specific legal requirements before launch.

Information you choose to provide

When you contact this business through the website, the information you submit may be used to respond to your request and provide the requested service.

Optional analytics

If analytics is enabled, this website may ask for consent before collecting limited anonymous engagement measurements such as page views, clicks, scroll depth, and supported-plan heatmap coordinates. Website hosting, communications, analytics, and other operational services may be provided by third-party processors configured by the business or NXQ-Web.

Your choices

You may decline optional analytics when prompted and contact the business regarding information you submitted through its website.
