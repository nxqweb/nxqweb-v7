# Local outreach kit: getting clients #2 to #10 in your own area (written 2026-10-18)

Owner decisions (2026-10-18): **any type of local business** (NXQX must not lean toward one industry), found **in your own area, in person and by phone**, leading with
**a free first month, then $50 to $150 a month**. Nothing here is automated: it is a script, a checklist and a tracking habit. Legal review is parked by the owner for later;
the wording below stays plain and promises only what exists.

## 1. Who qualifies (a 2-minute check, any industry)
Yes if ALL are true: it is a real local business with a phone number you can reach, it serves customers in or near your area, the owner or a decision-maker is reachable,
and its web presence is missing, out of date, not mobile friendly, or has no easy way to contact or book.
Skip (for now): chains and franchises, businesses that need a custom web app, anything you could not honestly help with, and anyone who says "no" twice.
Good signals: Facebook page but no website, a website that does not work on a phone, one phone number and no hours, few or no online reviews, "call to book" with no form.

## 2. Finding them near you (free)
- **Drive or walk a route** and note every storefront or van with a phone number but no web address. Take a photo of the sign for your notes.
- **Map search by hand:** search a category and your town in a maps app, open the businesses with no website link or a dead one.
- **Local social pages and community boards:** businesses that only post on social media.
- **Your own network and your current clients' referrals** (the Rewards page and referral link already exist; today nothing is paid out automatically).
Aim for a list of 30 to 50 before you start, in the tracker (section 8). Mixed industries is fine: the pitch below does not depend on the type.

## 3. In-person script (about 30 seconds)
> "Hi, I'm [name]. I build and look after websites for local businesses in [town]. I noticed [specific thing: no website / your site is hard to use on a phone]. I'd like to build you a simple
> one and look after it, and the first month is free so you can see it before you decide. Do you have two minutes, or is there a better time?"
- Ask the owner's name and the best way to reach them. Leave a one-page card (section 6). Do not push for a decision on the spot.
- If it is a bad time: "No problem. May I come back [day]?" and write it down.

## 4. Phone script (about 30 seconds)
> "Hi, is this [owner/manager name]? This is [name], a local web designer. I looked up [business] and [specific observation]. I help local businesses get a simple website that works on phones,
> and the first month is free so there's no risk. Would it be okay if I sent you a short example, or stopped by for five minutes this week?"
- One call, one clear next step (a text/email with the card, or a visit time). If they say not interested, thank them and mark it "no, do not contact again".
- Do not use recordings or automated dialing. Only message people who gave you their contact for that purpose.

## 5. The offer, in plain words
- **First month free.** You set up and run their site for 30 days at no charge.
- **Then** the plan they choose: **Starter $50/mo**, **Growth $100/mo**, **Intelligence $150/mo** (what each includes is on `/plans`). For bigger needs there is **NXQX Web Pro, from $300/mo, by request**.
- **Cancel any time.** No contract. Say it out loud.
- **They keep their business name, content and (if they have one) their domain.** Be exact about what you are promising; if a point is unclear, say you will confirm it in writing.
- **Put it in writing.** After a yes, send a short email that repeats: what is included, the free month's start and end dates, the price after, how to cancel. Both sides keep it.
- **How the free month works in the app today:** billing is by hand (Stripe invoices you create yourself), so a free month simply means no invoice until day 30. Put the invoice date in your calendar when they say yes. Nothing is discounted automatically.
- **Difference from the Founding-Client Program** (`docs/FOUNDING_CLIENT_PROGRAM_PLAN.md`): that plan is 5 testing spots at 50% off for 12 months and 10 free spots under written terms. A "free first month then paid" offer is simpler and is NOT that program. Decide whether this replaces it or sits beside it before you publish either on the site (the public founding text is already on the home page).

## 6. The leave-behind card (copy this onto a printed card or a text)

> First month free. After that $50 to $150 a month, cancel any time.
> See examples and plans: **[your site address]/plans**   ·   Call or text: **[your number]**
**Printable version built:** open **`/owner/outreach-card`** (owner screen, "Outreach card" link on the command center), type your name, number and website once, and press Print: six cards per sheet, each with a QR code that opens your plans page. Point a phone at the first card before printing a stack. The boxes are remembered in your browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.

## 7. Honest answers to common questions
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I already have a Facebook page." | "Good, keep it. A website is what shows up when someone searches, and your Facebook page can link to it." |
| "How much is it really?" | "The first month is free. Then $50, $100 or $150 depending on what you need, no contract." |
| "What's the catch?" | "There isn't one. I want a few good local examples. If you don't like it after a month you owe nothing." |
| "My nephew / a friend does websites." | "That's great. Mine is different because I keep it updated and handle changes for you every month. Happy to look at what you have." |
| "Who owns it?" | "Your business name, content and domain stay yours. I'll confirm exactly what that means in writing before we start." |
| "What if you disappear?" | "Fair question. I'll tell you plainly what happens to your site and your domain in the written summary, so you're never stuck." (Have this answer ready; do not improvise a promise.) |
| "I'm too busy." | "That's the point: it takes you about 10 minutes of questions and I do the rest." (Only say this if it is true for your intake form.) |
| "Not interested." | "Thanks for your time." Mark "no" and do not ask again. |

## 8. Tracking (use the app you already have)
The owner screen **`/owner/sales`** ("NXQX sales pipeline") already holds prospects with a niche, city, status and drafted outreach. Use it as your single list, and keep the notes to what
the prospect gave you (name, role, phone or email they chose to share, when to come back). Minimum columns if you use a plain sheet instead: business, owner name, how found, phone/email,
date contacted, result (no / call back / yes / pilot), next step and date, notes.
**Daily habit:** 10 first contacts, follow up on everyone due today, write down the result before you stop. Review once a week: how many contacts, conversations, yeses.
**Rough funnel to expect (GUESSES, replace with your real numbers after two weeks):** 20 first contacts -> 6 conversations -> 2 want to see something -> 1 yes.

## 9. After a yes (checklist)
1. Send the written summary email (section 5). 2. Point them to `/portal/signup` (or sign them up with them on your phone) and the intake form. 3. Calendar: day-30 review and invoice date.
4. Ask for one thing in return: an honest comment or a photo for their page, with their OK in writing. 5. Ask who else in their network might want the same (the referral link exists).
6. Never publish their story, name or numbers without their written OK.
