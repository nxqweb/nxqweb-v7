# Your store, in 5 minutes (a plain-words guide for the candle shop)

You do not need to know anything technical. Everything happens in your client portal, in the **Commerce** area. Words in **bold** are the buttons and menu names you will see.
The menu along the top of Commerce reads: **Dashboard, Tutorial, Products, Photos, Live store, Orders, Setup**, then **More pages**.

## 0. Before you start: photo format (iPhone)
Your store accepts **JPG, PNG, WEBP or GIF** photos, up to 8 per product. iPhones save photos as **HEIC** by default, and HEIC files are refused.
- **Easiest fix for next time:** on your iPhone open **Settings, Camera, Formats** and choose **Most Compatible**. New photos will be JPG.
- **Photos you already took:** when you pick them straight from your **Photos** library inside the portal, the iPhone usually converts them for you. Files sent as a "HEIC" attachment (for example through a chat app) will not work: send them again as a photo, or ask us to convert them.
- Keep each photo under 8 MB, in daylight, on a plain background, with one product per photo.

## 1. Add a product
1. Open **Commerce**, then **Products**.
2. In the **Add product** box fill in:
   - **Product name** (required), for example "Pink Peony Candle".
   - **Product type**, if the list has one that fits.
   - **Base price** (required).
   - **Short description** (one line) and **Full description** (a few sentences: scent, size, what it is made of).
   - **Price and stock**: the **Variant title** (for example "8 oz" or "Bar"), its **Price**, and **On hand** (how many you have).
3. Press **Save product draft**. The product is saved, but customers cannot see it yet.
4. Repeat for each candle, soap and wax melt. Only add facts you know are true.

## 2. Add its photos
1. Open **Photos** in the menu (not on the Products page).
2. Find your product and press **Upload photos**. You can add up to 8.
3. **The first photo is the main one** shown on your shop. Use the arrow buttons to move a photo, or **Remove** to delete one.
Photos stay private until you make the product visible (next step).

## 3. Show it in your shop
1. Open **Live store**.
2. Under **Product visibility**, press **Make visible** next to the product. (**Hide product** takes it back off the shop.)
3. Under **How do customers order?** pick one, then press **Save ordering choice**:
   - **Online, with a payment link:** customers place the order, then pay with your Venmo, PayPal or Stripe link. You only need ONE payment link.
   - **By email:** customers fill their cart and press **Email my order**. You get an email and reply with how to pay.
4. Press **Copy store link**. That link is your shop address: put it on your Instagram, business cards and emails.
Never type a password or secret key anywhere on this page.

## 4. When someone orders
Go to **Orders**. You will see the customer, what they bought and the total. Update the order status as you pack and ship it, and add a tracking note if you have one.
If you take payments by link, check your Venmo, PayPal or Stripe account to see the money arrive.

## 5. Get more reviews (if shown in your portal)
Open **Get reviews**, paste your Google review link, and press **Test my link**. You get a QR code and a ready-made card. Print it and put it in every package.

## If something looks wrong
Use the **Report a problem** button in the portal, or ask the assistant. Please do not worry about breaking anything: nothing you press here can charge a customer or move your money.
