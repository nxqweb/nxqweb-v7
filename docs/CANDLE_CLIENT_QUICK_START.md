# Your store, in 5 minutes (a plain-words guide for the candle shop)

You do not need to know anything technical. Everything happens in your client portal. Words in **bold** are the buttons you will see.

## 1. Add your candles and soaps
1. Open your portal and go to **Commerce**, then **Products**.
2. Press **Add product**. Type the name, a short description and the price.
3. Press **Save product draft**. A new product stays hidden from customers until you say so (step 3 below).
4. Go to **Photos** to add pictures. Clear, bright photos sell the most.

## 2. Check how customers will order
Open **Live store**. Under **How do customers order?** pick one:
- **Online, with a payment link:** customers place the order, then pay with your Venmo, PayPal or Stripe link.
- **By email:** customers fill their cart and press **Email my order**. You get an email and reply with how to pay.

Press **Save ordering choice**. You only need ONE payment link. Never type a password or secret key anywhere on this page.

## 3. Show products and open your store
On **Live store**, under **Product visibility**, press **Make visible** on each product you want customers to see.
Then open your store and press **Copy store link**. That link is your shop address: put it on your Instagram, business cards and emails.

## 4. When someone orders
Go to **Orders**. You will see the customer, what they bought and the total. Update the order status as you pack it and ship it, and add a
tracking note if you have one. If you take payments by link, check your Venmo, PayPal or Stripe account to see the money arrive.

## 5. Get more reviews (if shown in your portal)
Open **Get reviews**, paste your Google review link, and press **Test my link**. You get a QR code and a ready-made card. Print it and put it in
every package.

## If something looks wrong
Use the **Report a problem** button in the portal, or ask the assistant. Please do not worry about breaking anything: nothing you press here can
charge a customer or move your money.
