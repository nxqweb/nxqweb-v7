# Appointment requests: a 5-minute guide (early access)

Appointment requests let customers ask for a time on your website. **They are only requests.** Nothing is confirmed, paid for or reminded automatically. You decide and reply yourself.

## Set it up (client portal, "Appointment requests")
1. Press **Set up appointment requests**. Check your time zone.
2. **Services:** add what people can ask for and how long it takes (for example "Haircut, 45 minutes").
3. **Opening hours:** choose the days and hours you take appointments. Customers can only pick a time inside these hours.
4. **Page details:** a page name, a short intro, how much notice you need (default 2 hours) and how far ahead people can ask. You can add a cancellation note.
5. Press **Open my page**. Your public link looks like `.../book/your-name`. Put it on Instagram, business cards and your site.

## When someone asks
- The request shows in your portal with the person's name, email or phone, the time they asked for and their note. A request code is given to the customer.
- **Reply yourself** (call, text or email). Then set the status: Needs review, Declined or Cancelled, and add a note for yourself.
- A request is **not confirmed** until you tell the customer. The customer's page says so.

## Good to know
- Your customers' details are kept until you delete the request. Delete requests you no longer need.
- Spam brakes slow down abuse (a hidden trap, a limit per hour and per email or phone), but they are not full protection. If you get junk, delete it.
- No reminders, no payments, no calendar sync yet. Those are planned.
- Do not ask customers for card numbers, passwords or private health details in the note.
